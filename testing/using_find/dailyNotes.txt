So we come full circle. The problem we intiated bash learning for: comparison of text in pdf files for DFLs. The proposed solution was to place each word on it’s own line, and compare each word by line number and total line numbers. This is at play in the example provided in Learning "UNIX for OSx”, Dave Taylor, pg. 147
tr -cs “[:alpha:]” “\n” < alice.txt | uniq | wc -l
the above script would read every alpha numeric character, place them onto separate lines, remove non-unique characters, and then count the lines. This doesn’t exactly solve the problem we wanted to, but it’s a step in the right direction and proof we’re on the path.
