Bash 
Bash is a terminal emulation program used to navigate across computer systems. It has an immense amount of organizational power, as well as being the primary tool for System Administrators, and much more aside. This is well outside the scope of a graphic designer, and more within the realm of Computer Science Student. The main purpose of using it within the Graphics Department here at Select Corporation is to assure that data is being entered and maintained correctly, and to automate rote tasks that would otherwise take you lots of time and cost great sanity. 
It may seem cryptic at the start, and this has may be true generally. But the first time you do something that saves you hours, days, worth of repetitive typing, that studying time will have been worth it. For everyone including your employers.
