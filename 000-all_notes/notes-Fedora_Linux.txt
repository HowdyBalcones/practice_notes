>> Chapter 1 - Some history
	+ Fedora is a community run Linux distro sponsored by Red Hat
	+ started w/ Ken Thompson who wrote the first UNIX version, 
	it contained a kernel, a shell, an editor, and an assembler
	+ Richard Stallman started the GNU (GNU is Not Unix) around
	the idea of free software
	+ Linus Torvalds started the Linux project as a port of Unix to other 
	hardware in 1986. His original post to the mailing list is hilarious
	+ Linux is a kernel, GNU formed most of the utilities that surrounded the kernel
	+ By open sourcing Linux, people could port the GNU utils to other kernels
	+ Some other versions of Linux are Slackware, Debian, and Red Hat

	+ This is being written from a Fedora distribution. Fedora has a 6 month release schedule.
	Red Hat sponsors Fedora, but Red Hat has a 5 and 7 year release schedule for industry.
